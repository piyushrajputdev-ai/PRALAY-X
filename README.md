# PRALAY-X

> AI-Powered Disaster Digital Twin & Autonomous Rescue Intelligence System
> for Himalayan Disaster Management

PRALAY-X is an AI-driven disaster management platform designed to transform
disaster response from reactive decision-making into predictive,
simulation-based and intelligence-assisted response.

The system combines real-time environmental data, AI/ML risk assessment,
geospatial intelligence, disaster simulation, computer vision and rescue
optimization into a unified decision-support platform.

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Himalayan regions are highly vulnerable to:

- Cloudbursts
- Flash floods
- Landslides
- River overflow
- Road and bridge failures
- Remote-area isolation

Current disaster response systems often provide alerts without answering
critical operational questions:

- Which areas are likely to be affected next?
- Which roads may become inaccessible?
- Which communities may become isolated?
- What evacuation route should citizens follow?
- Which rescue team should be deployed first?
- What is the expected impact of a disaster under a specific scenario?

PRALAY-X addresses these gaps using predictive intelligence and
geospatial simulation.
